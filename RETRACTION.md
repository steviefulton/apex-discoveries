# Retraction notice — equation "discoveries" (2026-06-11)

The equation files previously published in this repository under
`equations/` were generated by the APEX Orchestrator's automated
discovery-and-publish pipeline **without adequate validation**.

A data-quality review on 2026-06-11 established that:

- the numerical extractor was producing artifact data points (a regex
  matched bare prose percentages as both dose and response), and
- the discovered equations were curve-fits to those artifacts or to
  synthetic seed fixtures, not to genuine external measurements.

**None of the published equations were grounded in verified external
data.** They are withdrawn and should not be cited or relied upon.

The publishing pipeline has been gated so that only equations grounded in
genuine external evidence can be published going forward; at present that
set is empty, which is the honest state.

— Steven Charles Fulton / APEX Orchestrator, 2026-06-11.
