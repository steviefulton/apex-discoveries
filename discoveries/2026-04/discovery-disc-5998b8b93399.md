## Hypothesis
Western paradigm locates amyloid clearance failure in static structural/fluid-dynamic deficits of the glymphatic apparatus itself. Russian paradigm locates it in dynamic sleep-state-dependent NK cell mobilization that *gates* glymphatic function. Divergence at: whether glymphatic dysfunction is a primary architectural pathology or a secondary consequence of sleep-dependent immune gating failure. Hypothesis: NK cell infiltration state (activated vs. quiescent) during NREM slow-wave sleep is the rate-limiting variable for amyloid clearance; glymphatic structural integrity is necessary but not sufficient. Testable by: measuring amyloid-β clearance kinetics in transgenic mice with chemically induced NK cell depletion versus wild-type controls, both sleep-restricted and sleep-replete, while simultaneously imaging glymphatic fluid dynamics and interstitial amyloid concentration. If NK depletion abolishes the sleep-dependent clearance advantage even in structurally intact glymphatic systems, the hypothesis is supported; if clearance remains sleep-dependent despite NK depletion, the Russian mechanism is incomplete.
